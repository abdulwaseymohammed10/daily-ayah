# Daily Ayah

Free iPhone Lock Screen wallpapers with a Quran verse in Arabic and English.

Open the app in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Pick verses and a background,
tap Generate, and follow the setup guide in the Save tab to make it change every day.

No account, app server, ads or tracking. Photos and settings stay on the device.

Quran text by the [Tanzil Project](https://tanzil.net), English translation by Saheeh
International. See [NOTICE.txt](NOTICE.txt).
